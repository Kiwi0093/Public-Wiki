## Preface

As a lifelong chubby guy, after being hospitalized for high blood pressure earlier this year, and thinking about my wife and kids, I figured I really ought to take losing weight seriously to prolong my life. After searching online for weight-loss methods, I found that over the past year or two, a ton of people have been promoting **_intermittent fasting_** for weight loss.

## Staged Fasting

> **Intermittent fasting involves strategically eating properly during the *eating* window and consuming zero calories during the *fasting* window, letting insulin rest while glucagon kicks in to break down fat.**

## Personal Experience

In my own case, after getting discharged from the hospital, I practiced a very-low-calorie diet, a low-carb diet, and 16/8 intermittent fasting. I once brought my weight down from 112.7 kg to 98 kg, and at the time it looked like it would keep dropping steadily..

However, I came back to Taiwan for a vacation, and between hotel quarantine / foodpanda / and the not-so-desperate efforts of the mom who spent nearly thirty years raising me into this body shape, my weight started rebounding rapidly. Then... I returned to Taiwan once again for the birth of my daughter, and my weight went right back up to 109 kg and stubbornly refused to budge.

I've basically been doing 16/8 intermittent fasting consistently, but maybe my physical condition really isn't great, and I eat too much during the eating window, so it's been hard to see sustained, effective fat loss. That's why I recently switched to the much more aggressive 23/1 intermittent fasting. On day one, it started dropping—from 109.6 kg → 107.8 kg. Of course, day one isn't reliable, so I plan to monitor it for at least a week to see how it goes.

## Conclusion

So far, intermittent fasting seems to be the most effective method I've tried. The main challenge going forward will be figuring out how to integrate it seamlessly into daily life—after all, the last time I broke my streak was due to a drastic change in lifestyle (was quarantine the main culprit for gaining weight?? Or was gorging myself during quarantine the main culprit??)
