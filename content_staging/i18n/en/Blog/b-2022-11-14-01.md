# Z-Library

### Overview

Based on the description on [Z-Library - Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia](https://zh.m.wikipedia.org/zh-tw/Z-Library), it actually sounds pretty positive.. but in reality it's a site all the major publishers couldn't wait to get rid of...

Basically it's a gigantic free e-book library.... and because of that, it got raided by the FBI....

### Current Status

Basically the web domain names were seized.. but something like this obviously won't truly disappear, so it went underground... Based on this login page that didn't get taken down:

[An account registration page I'm not sure needs to be kept low-key](https://zh.singlelogin.me/)

After registering an account, it gives you three options:

#### Tor

This "onion network" is to some extent the "dark web" that was hyped up like crazy a few years ago.... Though in practice it isn't actually that scary... It's mainly just because connecting to it requires a dedicated browser that it feels extra sneaky....

However, the desktop version of `Brave` that I use regularly actually has built-in Tor browsing... you just need to open a Private Window with Tor.... (Though based on my test today, it's pretty slow to respond... it takes a while to connect, probably because there are still far fewer nodes compared to the regular internet.)

#### I2P

Honestly this thing is pretty similar to Tor, just with even more emphasis on anonymity.... However... it really does require downloading a dedicated browser to use.... Personally, I'm not really a fan of tools that require installing extra software like that....

#### Telegram Bot

Honestly this really caught me by surprise... it's awesome....

Just add its bot (`@firstlibrarybot`) in your Telegram and link your account (instructions are right on its registration page, just follow along).

Then right inside that bot chat you can search for the books you want... and any file under 50MB can be downloaded directly in Telegram.. seriously generous.

# Conclusion

Honestly, this thing is basically skirting the edge of legality.. no idea how much longer it can survive underground like this... so if you want any books, it's probably smarter to grab them while you can.....
