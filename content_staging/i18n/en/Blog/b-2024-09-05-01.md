## TubeArchivist

[TubeArchivist](https://www.tubearchivist.com/) is an offline YouTube archiver. Its basic function is subscribing to a YouTube channel and downloading all the videos from that channel for offline viewing...

Moreover, it can integrate and serve those files to `emby`/`plex`/`jellyfin` as library media for playback.

## TubeArchivist-Plugin for metadata

Even though TubeArchivist downloads the actual video files, the filenames are based on YouTube IDs rather than human-readable metadata.
So people developed plugins to pull YouTube metadata and convert it for `emby/jellyfin` so that what you see looks proper.

### [TubeArchivist-emby](https://github.com/xinmans/tubearchivist-emby)

Because `emby` doesn't have it implemented as a plugin, you have to attach an external Python service to convert it. I spent an entire morning on it and still couldn't get it working, and I didn't feel like debugging Python code... so I stamped it "Abandoned."

### [TubeArchivist-JF-Plugin](https://github.com/tubearchivist/tubearchivist-jf-plugin)

Just to make it work, I even went and installed `jellyfin`, and it succeeded! The gotchas are:

* After installing the plugin, remember to restart Jellyfin, otherwise the plugin won't initialize.

* After configuring and saving the plugin settings, remember to restart Jellyfin again, otherwise the connection will keep failing... and it won't be able to update metadata.
