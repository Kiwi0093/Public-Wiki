# Story Outline

## How It Started

So here's what happened: one day while I was slacking off at work, I suddenly noticed the storage pool on one of my home NAS boxes had vanished. Looking closer, the PCI-passthrough SATA expansion card had suddenly died...

## Emergency Response

Although rebooting ESXi would temporarily bring the expansion card back to life before it died again after a while, I acted decisively and immediately ordered a new SATA expansion card (and threw in some other stuff on PChome that cost more than the card itself). Currently still waiting for delivery.

## Emergency Response II - The Idle Gen8

Since I also have an idle Gen8 sitting at home, last night I tried harvesting parts from the Gen8 to see if I could fix it. Long story short, while it's also a 4-port card and the card itself works, its 1-to-4 breakout cable has issues (probably), so out of four drives it could only detect three.... still a fail in the end..

## Emergency Response III - The Active Gen8

Then I turned my attention to the active Gen8, thinking the idle card still had at least two working ports so swapping them might work.... Then I opened up the machine and realized how naive and silly I was...

The SATA expansion card in that machine... is a 2-port card.... damn it!! (I really cut corners back then.)

## Current Status

What else can I do? Right now I can only shut down all services bound to the dead NAS... unplug power to the HDDs, and wait until the new card arrives at home.

## The Infuriating Part

# Future Plans...

Later I chatted with my mentor, which shattered quite a few of my existing assumptions:

* If you haven't bought vMotion, or don't want to switch to Proxmox VE, it's still better to consolidate VMs onto a single machine..
  
  Thinking about it carefully, as long as you beef up the hardware on one VM host, there's really no need to spread ESXi across the other two Gen8s.. Running ESXi with only 16GB RAM is pretty useless anyway...

* Just use your Gen8 as a pure NAS..
  
  That's actually true, because a Gen8 only has one PCIe slot, and eventually it'll need a 10Gb NIC.... I originally wanted to do iSCSI and whatnot, but switching back to a pure NAS means the OS basically just needs a USB stick, leaving all the hardware resources for the NAS.

* Ditch Raidz1 and just use ZFS mirror (formerly RAID 1) instead
  
  Right now HDD prices per GB for anything under 8TB are basically dirt cheap/not worth it, so the recommendation is to go straight for 16TB HDDs x2. Currently two 16TB drives cost around 19k NTD, so in a 4-bay machine you can gradually build two 16TB pools.

Given my current situation and budget, this seems like the better option—after all, just rebuilding the 10Gb backbone, Wi-Fi, and buying a base server is already super expensive (almost 100k NTD). Trying to go all-in all at once right now would be stretching way too thin.

Plus, if I cheap out and fill all four bays with 4TB drives right now.. there'll be no room for future expansion anyway.. going straight for larger HDDs is definitely better..
