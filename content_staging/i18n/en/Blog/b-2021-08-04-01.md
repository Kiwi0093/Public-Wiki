# Preface

After moving back to Taiwan, sharing files with friends in China like I used to became more troublesome and difficult... so I thought about using a forum + cloud drive approach for sharing.


<!--truncate-->


# Basic Concept

Actually, a cloud drive alone is enough for sharing files, but constantly passing download links back and forth over mobile IM apps—especially when not everyone is in the same chat group (or even using different IM platforms)—

is really a hassle. So I wanted to set up a place to post links. If everything were shared solely by me, I could probably just write something like a blog to list them out.

Unfortunately, the main people doing the sharing aren't really that geeky.... So I figured I'd set up a simple platform, and after shopping around I picked phpBB to run a forum for it.

(Pretty much the same idea as places like Light Novel Kingdom or 3DM.)

# phpBB

<font  size="+2"  color="blue">Why choose phpBB?</font>

Good question. Actually I didn't really pick it carefully—mostly it's because back in college I set up a very old version of phpBB, though back then my conclusion was that it felt less convenient than a traditional BBS (because it was slow).

Later on, though, these PHP-based forums popped up everywhere and the software got comparatively better.

Then why didn't I pick Discuz, which dominated the Greater China region?

Even though there are tons of comparisons online with fans on both sides, the real reason for choosing it is way simpler than all that..

<font  size="+3"  color="darkgreen">Because phpBB has a Docker image you can spin up effortlessly~~~</font>

Yes, all of it comes from my lazy desire to solve everything with Docker—that simple, that unpretentious of a reason.

As usual, the detailed setup steps will go into the Wiki.

# Free Cloud Storage

Speaking of this, it's honestly a bit awkward. Nowadays there are plenty of free cloud drives available, yet sharing across Taiwan and China narrows the choices down a lot.

The ones commonly used in Taiwan either require a VPN to reach from China or are painfully slow.

The ones commonly used in China generally offer huge space and fast speeds inside China, but the account registration process makes anyone in Taiwan want to back out immediately.

Later, while downloading novels, I stumbled upon one where registration only needs an email, gives 500GB of free space, requires no login to download, and has decent speeds in both China and Taiwan:

<font  size="+2"  color="blue">[Chengtong Netdisk (ctfile)](https://www.ctfile.com/)</font>

No idea how long this thing will last either, anyway that's how free hosting goes—everyone just uses it while it works, and if it dies we'll find another one.
