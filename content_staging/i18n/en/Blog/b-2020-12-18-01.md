Last time, Bliss OS, Phoenix OS, and even vanilla Android-x86 all had issues where APKs couldn't be installed directly (mainly because the lack of Google signatures prevented installation). Although I looked at plenty of guides on using `adb` to install APKs, in actual testing Bliss OS on the Surface Pro 4 frequently suffered from touch panel failures and other issues... so I had to look for another way out...

<!--truncate-->

## Fyde OS

Basically, this isn't Android—it's a *ChromeOS* derivative created by a Chinese vendor. However, because ChromeOS can also install APKs to run Android apps, and installing it is simpler than installing regular ChromeOS—plus it claims to have an image specifically tailored for the Surface Pro 4—I downloaded it to give it a try.

### Official Info

[Official Website](https://fydeos.com/)

### Installation Method

Just download the image matching your device on the [Download Page](https://fydeos.com/download), flash it onto a USB drive using [Etcher](https://www.balena.io/etcher/), boot from the USB, and finish running the installer...

### User Experience

So far I've confirmed that ChromeOS apps can be installed, Google Play can be installed, and Android apps can be installed via Google Play / Wandoujia / standalone APKs. Basic drivers work fine too, and the owner of the machine is currently doing further testing (to see if it crashes easily or runs into any weird issues...). What's already known is that intense use of the touch panel causes the touch panel driver to crash and stop responding... So if you want to play Android games with this, just go get a Bluetooth controller...
