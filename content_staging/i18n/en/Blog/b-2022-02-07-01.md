# The True Form of the Tragedy

## Can't receive emails in my inbox..

What happened was such a coincidence—I wouldn't even have noticed that my inbox had stopped receiving emails...
until one day I foolishly wanted to play HOI4 on Linux (since game streaming kept running into issues, I just brute-forced running it directly on Linux).
Then I forgot my Steam password, tried to reset it, and kept never receiving the email...
And that's when I realized, crap, the mailbox can't receive mail anymore...

## Steam Solution

Used my phone number... reset the password and then changed the email address...

## LinkedIn Solution

Used my phone number... changed the password and then swapped out both the email and phone number (otherwise having to keep turning on my China SIM card all the time is really exhausting..)

## Oracle Cloud

I already knew from last time that it was hopeless... but today got even worse—one of the accounts got locked.... and of all things, it locked the one I was actually using...
After whining to customer support, in the afternoon I noticed that the Indian support rep actually swapped my locked account over to my Osaka account that had never been able to create a VM...
Guess that counts as a stroke of luck.....

# Conclusion

Linking a phone number really does come in handy... otherwise today would have been completely unsolvable.....
