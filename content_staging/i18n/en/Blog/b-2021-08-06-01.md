Previously I tried setting up Disqus and an unsuccessful DisqusJS, but honestly it didn't look great, so I switched to Isso. Though even with that, nobody's going to leave comments on a diary-style blog like this anyway...

# Isso

This is a self-hosted comment system, yet it's very lightweight, and the Hexo-theme-NexT I'm using has built-in support for it. While searching around for usable comment systems, none of the others felt quite right, so I decided to go with this one.

* <font  size="+1"  color="darkgreen">Supports anonymous comments</font> I figured nobody commented probably because logging in to comment was too much hassle.. ~~actually it's just that nobody reads it...~~
* <font  size="+1"  color="darkgreen">Supports Markdown</font> So I can add notes on my own posts.. ~~how lonely do you have to be to comment notes on your own blog...~~
* <font  size="+1"  color="darkgreen">Accessible without a VPN</font> Unless the Great Firewall goes crazy and blocks even Cloudflare's CDN.
