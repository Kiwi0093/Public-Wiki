# Background

Originally I bought my domain from [Freenom](https://www.freenom.com/en/index.html?lang=en), and later due to issues on their platform, their management rights for `.com` domains were revoked, so it was transferred directly to [Gandi](https://www.gandi.net/zh-Hant).
Actually, Gandi is also a very large DNS registrar with pretty solid services, but their renewal fee is just too expensive—about 37 USD per year... (my original price on Freenom was around 10 USD per year).
So I migrated over to the much cheaper [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/zh-tw/).

# Cloudflare DNS Registrar

Cloudflare is a huge internet services company. A long time ago I had already pointed my DNS to Cloudflare for management; their free tier alone is already great to use.

## Mail Alias

Right now, my domain's email uses Zoho's service, though honestly I don't have the greatest impression of it...
Cloudflare actually lets you alias domain emails to Gmail; after some setup, besides receiving mail, you can also send mail using your own domain:

* [HalisUnreal's Blog](https://blog.halisunreal.com/posts/use-your-own-domain-email/)
* [Configuring a Custom Domain Email on Gmail Using Cloudflare](https://mskclover.com/2024/07/31/custom-email-on-gmail-by-cloudflare/)
* [Sending and Receiving Custom Domain Email with Cloudflare + Gmail](https://www.waterwater.moe/posts/2024/2024-01-01_%E4%BD%BF%E7%94%A8-cloudflare--gmail-%E5%AE%8C%E6%88%90%E8%87%AA%E5%AE%9A%E4%B9%89%E5%9F%9F%E5%90%8D%E9%82%AE%E7%AE%B1%E6%94%B6%E5%8F%91/)

Later on, I might still consider migrating email too just to cut down on having too many service platforms. Anyway, since it can map to any Gmail account, I can just register a random Gmail account and map it there.

That makes it easy to set up email addresses for family members as well.
