# Preface

Since Typora started charging earlier, I switched to TextMark as my new Markdown Editor. Although there wasn't anything wrong with it, seeing someone use Obsidian for note-taking made me want to switch over as well.

<!--truncate-->

# Basic Usage
It's actually very straightforward because Obsidian is a cross-platform note-taking app that can be installed directly regardless of which OS you use. You just open an existing directory and point it to your current Git repo location.

# What Stood Out
Honestly.. I haven't seen it yet... Maybe it's because I don't really need its strongest feature—backlinks—yet?
Also, some people love Obsidian's graph view, enjoying the sight of a bunch of nodes connected together and feeling like their knowledge is all linked up too.
However, when I used Obsidian to directly open the notes I had written before... they were all isolated nodes with nothing connected....
Maybe it needs extra setup later, though considering I don't read my notes directly in Obsidian—instead converting them via Hexo into a static website and pushing them to GitHub—that probably won't be useful anyway...
