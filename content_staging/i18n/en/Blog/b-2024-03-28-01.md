## Cangshu / Shuipen Lamb Soup (Knockoff Version)

During the years I lived in Suzhou, whenever winter came around, I'd often go with coworkers for a bowl of Cangshu lamb soup. For someone like me who dislikes the gamey smell of mutton, it was one of the few lamb dishes I actually liked (along with lamb skewers).

When I went back to visit my wife's family last year, I also brought back some stewing spice packs for Shuipen lamb soup, so I made this:
![Knockoff Cangshu / Shuipen Lamb Soup Mix](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kiwi0093/graph/master/img/lamp.jpg)

The reason it's a knockoff is that Cangshu lamb soup doesn't use stewing spices, and Shuipen lamb soup doesn't have napa cabbage and the like...
I literally just threw them both together.

Then, when friends came over for a housewarming celebration, I made this again:

![Lamb Soup + Dry Pot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kiwi0093/graph/master/img/lamp+chicken01.jpg)

Though I later realized you really shouldn't put dry pot and lamb soup together in a yin-yang hotpot... otherwise the dry side burns...
