# Story Summary

Right while I was recently wrestling with `Drone CI`—yet another tool I was forced to give up on due to lack of skill....

I suddenly realized my `/var/lib/docker/volumes` directory was empty.....

Thinking back, could it have been caused a month or two ago when I got itchy fingers and used `portainer` to clean up `unused` directories in the volume library... If so, it's pretty impressive that my internal services kept running normally for one or two months....

# Scope of Impact

Actually it's nothing special—just that all internal services got wiped out.... Looks like today's afternoon will be great practice for rebuilding everything from scratch.....

# Remediation

All I can say is thank goodness I kept all my `docker-compose.yml` files backed up beforehand, so the services just need to be rebuilt.... and that's it... (having to redo the painful configuration all over again, though).

# Silver Lining

The only thing that counts as a silver lining is that because I had to reconfigure everything, I went and read the docs for these services and discovered they added new features in newer versions that I can put to use right now (finding joy in misery, I guess....).
