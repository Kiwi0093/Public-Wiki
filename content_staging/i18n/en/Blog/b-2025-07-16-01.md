# Preface

Following that previous Archlinux crash incident I thought had happened, I got trolled by KDE Plasma yet again....

<!--truncate-->

# Tragedy Strikes Again!?

A great man haunted by a scumbag spirit once said, "People keep repeating the same mistakes... good grief!"
Yes, once again I got trigger-happy with `yay -Syu --noconfirm` followed by a bunch of `sync` commands and `sudo reboot`.
And then my VNC couldn't connect again.... Checked it out and found that my `plasma-x11` option had vanished...

# The Truth Is Always One!

Yep, the culprit was KDE Plasma... Digging through a bunch of posts, I discovered that starting from Plasma 6.4, the X11 session was removed from the default installation...
So I started hunting for a `KDE + Wayland` remote desktop solution again...

Then, right as I was trying out `krdp` and getting frustrated with its performance... I discovered:

## `Turns out you can manually install the X11 session~~~~`

So without hesitation, I slapped the machine with a `yay -S  plasma-x11-session --noconfirm`.
And the problem is ~~solved~~ postponed until Plasma 7 comes around.

# Ramblings

Although I know the whole community has been shouting that Xorg is too old and pushing to gradually migrate to Wayland..
Right now, in practice, I really haven't found a remote desktop solution that feels more efficient than `vnc`.. Maybe in the not-too-distant future,
I'll switch everything over to `moonlight/sunshine`, because all the current `rdp` solutions on Linux feel pretty sluggish to use—
nowhere near as smooth as Windows....
