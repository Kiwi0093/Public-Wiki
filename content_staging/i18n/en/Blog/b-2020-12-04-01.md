### Key Points (Without the Fluff)

Simply put, I applied the `case` loop I learned yesterday and merged what used to look like several fragmented installation scripts into one where the user only has to type a single command and pick from a menu to finish installing any of my preset setups:

* Plain Arch Linux - Honestly, this setup will probably be the script I use most often for testing things.. because nothing extra is installed; it's just something that boots up and has networking.
* Arch Linux with Nextcloud pre-installed - This one will probably see the least use, and its contents differ the most from the other scripts... (and it'll likely be the very last one I finish—~~assuming I don't get lazy and just cut this option altogether~~)
