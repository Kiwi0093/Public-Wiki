## Berwick Saga

This is a turn-based strategy game similar to Fire Emblem (since the creator originally worked on FE before leaving to make this series, which led to a whole mess of disputes with Nintendo—won't go into that).

[The Japanese Wikipedia page is here](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%86%E3%82%A3%E3%82%A2%E3%83%AA%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B0%E3%82%B5%E3%83%BC%E3%82%AC%E3%82%B7%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BA_%E3%83%99%E3%83%AB%E3%82%A6%E3%82%A3%E3%83%83%E3%82%AF%E3%82%B5%E3%83%BC%E3%82%AC)

For me, what makes it so fun isn't just the cavalry system where **_horses are consumables_**, but also the fact that this is a game where **_even the protagonist rides a horse_** and everyone goes full cavalry ~~high-speed movement~~ together (alright, it's weird little quirks like this that hit my sweet spot), plus it has PS2-era graphics (though playing it now, it really does look pretty bare-bones~~).

After finishing this playthrough, I'm planning to go back and replay the PS1 predecessor - **TearRing Saga: Utna Heroes Saga**. Apparently that one has a dual-protagonist system.

## PCSX2
