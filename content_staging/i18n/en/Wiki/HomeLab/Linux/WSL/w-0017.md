## Main Role

* #### Hope for escaping corporate IT system shackles
* #### Important node for managing `HomeLab` from the office
* #### VPN Console connecting back home from the office

As the name implies, `WSL` is `Linux running on Windows`. Through WSL, I can install tools that my company does not allow me to install, and connect back home via VPN to use it as a springboard or directly as a terminal.
Naturally, the distribution running inside WSL is my favorite—`Arch Linux`.

## Major Service

* #### WireGuard
* #### Desktop Tools
* #### Remote Desktop Client
