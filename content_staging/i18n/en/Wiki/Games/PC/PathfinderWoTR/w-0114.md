### Implementation SOP: Body Snatching Method (Precise Organ Transplant)

To avoid bugs like level-up UI crashes and respec vaporization, we must borrow a legitimate pet's "household registration" (slot) and graft the mercenary's "body."

**Step 1: Obtain Registration and Body**

Hire a brand new mercenary at the tavern in-game, setting up appearance and class. Open ToyBox, navigate to `Party` -> `Features`, and forcibly grant the master an animal feat they would never normally use (e.g., centipede) to let the system legally generate a pet. Change maps to spawn the entity, then save and exit.

**Step 2: Free Up Party Space**

Extract the `.zks` save and open `player.json`. Locate the `"m_Party"` array and remove the mercenary's UUID from it.

**Step 3: Data Porting and Overwriting**

Open `party.json` and locate the block for the "centipede (legitimate pet)."

Take `"Doll"` (3D appearance), `"m_Inventory"` (equipment slots), `"m_Slots"` (equipment nodes), and `"Stats"` (base attributes) from the mercenary block, and **overwrite the corresponding blocks of the centipede**. Never overwrite the centipede's `"Blueprint"` and `"Progression"` (animal progression trees must be retained). Once done, completely delete the original mercenary's large standalone block.

**Step 4: Master-Servant Bidirectional Binding**

In the `"Descriptor"` block of the synthesized entity (the original centipede), locate the `"Master"` field and enter the master's UUID. Search for the master's name, find the `"m_Pets"` array, and confirm the synthesized entity's UUID is present within it. Pack the JSON back into `.zks` and enter the game.
