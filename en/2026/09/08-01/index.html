OCI Free Tier Terms Change

September 8, 2026 · 4 min read
info

This article was automatically translated by Google "Gemini". The author does not guarantee its accuracy or wording.

While browsing around today, I noticed that OCI's Free Tier terms changed. Looking closer, they actually sent out a notice on June 15 this year.. but I never pay much attention to OCI's emails...

OCI Always Free A1 Resources Before vs. After Update​

Resource Item🔄 Before Aug 2026 Update🚨 After Aug 2026 Update (Latest Policy)💡 Account Restructuring & Ops Impact
Max OCPU per Account4 OCPU2 OCPU (Cut in half)Excess instances will be automatically terminated (Terminate) by the system.
Memory (RAM) per Account24 GB12 GB (Cut in half)Max quota per single VM drops to 12 GB; combined total across multiple VMs cannot exceed this either.
Monthly Free Usage Limit3,000 OCPU hours / month
18,000 GB hours / month		1,500 OCPU hours / month
9,000 GB hours / month		PAYG (Pay-As-You-Go) users usually won't have VMs deleted for exceeding the limit, but overages will be billed directly to the credit card.
Max VM Combination Examples• 1x 4C/24G large VM
• 4x 1C/6G micro VMs		• 1x 2C/12G full-spec VM
• 2x 1C/6G standalone VMs		Resource reshuffle: ditch fragmented tiny VMs and consolidate compute to get the most value.
Free Block Storage (Total Disk)200 GB200 GB (Unchanged)Although disk wasn't shrunk, splitting across 2 VMs leaves very little space per VM—still a storage pain point.
Free Outbound Public Traffic (Network)10 TB / month10 TB / month (Unchanged)Bandwidth allowance remains super generous, great for acting as a high-bandwidth relay or sharing space.

Impact​

Honestly, even though my OCI setup looks super fancy right now—with a WireGuard mesh and all that—the only services I actually use are:

  • Wireguard for BT download - this is to keep copyright trolls off my back
  • V2Ray via 3X-UI - this is for friends still in Chinathe bandit zone Everything else is just flashy fluff that makes no difference if wiped out.

I checked carefully and haven't been charged yet, and it seems PAYG isn't being strictly enforced right now either, though with a trust shift like this it's best not to gamble on a corporation's goodwill.... Better to proactively downgrade myself for peace of mind.

New Plan​

Honestly I haven't thought it through completely yet, but my rough plan is as follows: My current three accounts will probably be split up like this:

Account A A-1 1C6G running the current WireGuard role (basically keeping its current scale and purpose) joined to the mesh A-2 1C6G for running 3X-UI and things provided to friends The nice thing about this is for security settings, only Account A needs ports 80/443 exposed externally, while the other accounts stay open only within the Mesh.

Accounts B & C B 2C12G I don't know what to do with it yet, but join it to the mesh C 2C12G I don't know what to do with it yet, but join it to the mesh

With the specs cut in half, AI workloads that barely couldn't run before definitely can't run now.... so I have even less idea what to use them for.... Still, I'll probably reorganize things first.

Bonus​

Since Watchtower has been acting weird lately, I plan to switch to a systemd timer triggering docker compose up -d to automatically update my Docker images. Once I get that set up, I'll update my wiki with it as well.
